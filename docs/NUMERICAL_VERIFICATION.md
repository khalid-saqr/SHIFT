# Phase 2 numerical verification and uncertainty

Phase 2 turns the exact source04 mechanics kernel into an auditable numerical reconstruction layer. It does not introduce a closure experiment, WSS-pair search, or universal numerical acceptance tolerance.

## Numerical support convention

Structured scalar fields have trailing spatial shape `(nx, ny, nz)`. Structured vector fields have trailing shape `(nx, ny, nz, 3)`. A leading time axis is allowed. Cartesian coordinates are stored explicitly in metres and must be finite and strictly increasing.

The velocity-gradient convention remains

$$
A_{ij}=\frac{\partial u_i}{\partial x_j}.
$$

## Differential operators

`shift.operators` implements local polynomial finite differences directly from the supplied coordinate values. No uniform-grid assumption is required.

- first derivatives use three-point local stencils;
- second derivatives use four-point local stencils;
- one-sided boundary stencils are generated by the same polynomial-weight construction;
- spatial gradient, divergence, curl, scalar Laplacian, and vector Laplacian are composed from those operators;
- temporal derivatives use the explicit time coordinate;
- wall-normal derivatives require a distance coordinate beginning at zero at the wall and increasing into the fluid.

The declared target is second-order convergence on smooth systematically refined fields. Tests use nonuniform polynomial fields for exactness and smooth trigonometric fields for observed-order verification.

## Numerical source04 reconstruction

`shift.numerics.reconstruct_source04` accepts sampled velocity and pressure fields and reconstructs the primary source04 quantities using the Phase-2 operators.

Hydrodynamic charge is evaluated by its defining route

$$
q_H=\nabla\cdot(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf u),
$$

while the independent identity route

$$
q_H^{(I)}=-2Q-\nabla^2K
$$

is retained separately. Their difference is the charge-identity residual.

For absent or supplied conservative body-force potential, the Crocco components are evaluated as

$$
\mathbf C_U=\frac{\partial\mathbf u}{\partial t},
$$

$$
\mathbf C_H=\nabla\left(\frac{p}{\rho}+K+\Phi\right),
$$

and, for constant-density Newtonian flow,

$$
\mathbf C_D=-\nu\nabla^2\mathbf u.
$$

The existing source04 certification layer then evaluates continuity, charge, and Crocco residuals. A steady state must be declared explicitly; SHIFT does not silently replace an unavailable temporal derivative by zero.

## Independent verification fields

### Polynomial manufactured fields

Polynomial fields verify the finite-difference coefficients independently of source04 mechanics. The selected polynomial degrees are reproduced to floating-point precision by the declared local stencils.

### Smooth convergence field

For a sinusoidal scalar field, refinement over four grids verifies that both first- and second-derivative RMS errors approach at least second-order convergence on the finest three levels.

### Taylor-Green vortex

The dimensional Taylor-Green vortex in `shift.models.taylor_green` is an exact incompressible Navier-Stokes solution. It supplies time-resolved velocity and pressure fields with known dynamical consistency. Refinement tests require the numerical continuity, hydrodynamic-charge, and Crocco residuals to decrease under refinement.

Taylor-Green is a verification solution. It is not an arterial model and is not used as evidence for or against WSS closure.

### Parallel-plate Poiseuille wall derivative

The Phase-1 Poiseuille profile is quadratic in wall-normal distance. The one-sided wall-normal operator therefore recovers the exact lower-wall velocity gradient to roundoff on an irregular wall-normal stencil. Feeding that gradient through the Phase-1 Newtonian stress and traction implementation recovers the locked wall shear stress exactly.

## Convergence-derived uncertainty

`shift.uncertainty` separates three concepts.

A `ConvergenceSeries` records characteristic spacing and independently known error over at least three refinement levels. Its observed order is the slope of

$$
\log E = p\log h + c.
$$

For a quantity evaluated on a coarse and fine discretization, a verified order $p$ can then be used for a Richardson estimate of the fine-grid discretization uncertainty,

$$
U_h=\frac{|f_h-f_{rh}|}{r^p-1},
\qquad
r=\frac{h_{\mathrm{coarse}}}{h_{\mathrm{fine}}}>1.
$$

The value of $p$ is supplied from an independently verified operator/convergence study. SHIFT does not fit $p$ to obtain a desired qualification result.

An `UncertaintyBudget` combines declared absolute uncertainty bounds conservatively by addition. Spatial, temporal, wall-gradient, pressure-gradient, interpolation, or other components can therefore remain separate and auditable.

A numerical threshold created by `threshold_from_budget` is exactly equal to the declared budget. No hidden multiplier, 5% convention, percentile substitution, or post-outcome relaxation is applied.

## Qualification

`IdentityThresholds` contains provenance-bearing thresholds for continuity, charge, and Crocco residuals. `qualify_identity_residuals` performs only the comparison

$$
R_j\le U_j.
$$

The function never changes the supplied residual or threshold. A residual above its uncertainty-derived bound fails qualification.

This design preserves the source04 rule that numerical admissibility is established before any WSS-equivalence or mechanical-state outcome is inspected.
